Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 October 2026

Seher is a prayer times, Quran, hadith, Qibla and dhikr app. It has no user accounts and no server of its own. Everything you enter or record in the app stays on your phone. This policy explains what the app uses, what leaves your phone, and the choices you have.

1. Data that stays on your phone

The following is stored only on your device and is never sent to us:

  • Your selected city or location used to calculate prayer times
  • Prayer time settings, notification and sound preferences, theme and language
  • Prayer tracking records (prayed / missed / make-up), dhikr counts and your own dhikrs
  • Quran bookmarks, last read position and favourite hadiths

We cannot see, access or restore this data. Deleting the app deletes it.

2. Location

If you allow location access, your position is used on your phone to calculate prayer times and the Qibla direction. It is not sent to us. You can instead pick a city by hand and deny location access.

When you open Nearby mosques, the app sends your approximate coordinates to the public OpenStreetMap Overpass service (overpass-api.de, or one of its public mirrors if it is busy) to find mosques around you. The map itself is displayed by Apple Maps on iOS (or Google Maps on Android), which receives the map area being shown. These services handle the request under their own privacy policies; we do not receive or keep this information.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

Seher is free and is funded by ads served by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect:

  • Device identifiers such as the advertising identifier (IDFA on iOS), only if you allow tracking
  • IP address and approximate (coarse) location derived from it
  • Ad interaction and app usage data, and diagnostic data such as crash and performance information

On iOS you are asked whether the app may track you (App Tracking Transparency). If you decline, the advertising identifier is not shared and ads are not personalised. In the EEA, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, a Google consent form asks for your choice before personalised ads are shown; you can change it later under Settings → Ad & privacy choices. Ads are limited to content rated for general audiences, and no ads are shown in the Quran, Qibla, dhikr and prayer tracking screens or at prayer time.

Your location used for prayer times, your worship records and your notes are never shared with advertisers.

Google's use of data is described at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites and policies.google.com/privacy.

4. “Remove Ads” purchase

The one-time “Remove Ads” purchase is processed entirely by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). We do not receive your payment details. The app only stores on your phone that the purchase is active. After the purchase, the advertising SDK is no longer started.

5. Notifications

Prayer time, reminder and daily hadith notifications are scheduled locally on your phone. No push notification service or server is used.

6. Children

Seher is a general-audience app and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

7. Your rights and choices

  • Turn location access off at any time in your phone's settings.
  • Change tracking permission in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Change ad consent in the app under Settings → Ad & privacy choices (where required).
  • Delete all app data by deleting the app.

Because we do not hold your personal data, we cannot export or delete it on our side. For requests about data held by Google, use Google's tools linked above. Residents of Türkiye can read our KVKK information notice.

8. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.
